👑 The Forgotten King

DLC I • Eight Chapters • Prequel

Before the darkness consumed the world, there was a peaceful kingdom ruled by King Aldric.

When an ancient purple crystal is discovered beneath the castle, the king becomes obsessed with its promise of power.

Play as Sir Aeron, Aldric's loyal knight, and witness the tragedy that creates the Purple King.

Timeline importance: This is where the Black Spirit's influence over King Aldric begins. It explains the origin of the Purple King.

Main Story • Eight Chapters

Centuries after the fall of King Aldric's kingdom, villagers begin disappearing under mysterious circumstances.

A new hero must journey through forests, frozen mountains, dangerous lands, and the darkness below.

The adventure eventually leads to a confrontation with the Purple King.
