TheDarkWithin

A peaceful village... until they were taken.

Travel through snowy mountains, mysterious forests, and scorching deserts. Find the missing villagers and face the evil that waits in the darkness.

CLICK Select and interact.

WASD or arrow keys: Move.

SPACE Punch monsters.

Watch your health and keep moving!
